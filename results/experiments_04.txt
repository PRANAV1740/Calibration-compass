CalibrationCompass - Experiment 4

Experiment:
Multi-circuit fidelity benchmark

Circuits:
GHZ
QFT-like
QAOA-like
Hardware-efficient

Backends:
FakeSherbrooke
FakeTorino
FakeFez

Results:

GHZ:
Sherbrooke = 0.911096
Torino = 0.751756
Fez = 0.943208
Winner = Fez

QFT-like:
Sherbrooke = 0.999543
Torino = 0.998637
Fez = 0.998643
Winner = Sherbrooke

QAOA-like:
Sherbrooke = 0.993696
Torino = 0.994210
Fez = 0.996194
Winner = Fez

Hardware-efficient:
Sherbrooke = 0.989674
Torino = 0.953178
Fez = 0.997277
Winner = Fez

Main observation:
Different circuit structures can have different backend performance.
The results also show that circuit depth and two-qubit gate count
alone do not completely determine execution fidelity.
