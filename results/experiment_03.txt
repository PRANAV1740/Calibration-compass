CalibrationCompass - Experiment 3

Synthetic calibration drift sweep

Qubit 1

Main observation:
The optimal backend changes as hardware calibration conditions
change. Fez is best under normal conditions, but Sherbrooke
becomes better after sufficient degradation of Fez qubit 1.
